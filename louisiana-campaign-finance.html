About & Methodology

How this dashboard is built, where the numbers come from, and what they do — and don't — mean.

What this is

An independent, public-interest dashboard for exploring Louisiana campaign contributions, expenditures, and loans. It is not an official government site and is not affiliated with the Louisiana Board of Ethics. It re-presents public records to make them easier to search and understand. For official records, always consult ethics.la.gov.

Where the data comes from

Board of Ethics
Contributions, expenditures, and loans come from the Louisiana Board of Ethics bulk campaign-finance data downloads (filers report electronically).
Certified reports
Cash-on-Hand figures are read from each filer's certified annual reports (F102 candidate / F202 PAC). These are the only true balance figures shown, and each links back to its source filing.
Secretary of State
Candidate party, ballot appearances, and election results are enriched from Louisiana Secretary of State records.

How often it updates

Transaction data refreshes nightly from the Board of Ethics downloads; certified Cash-on-Hand is scraped incrementally as new annual reports are filed. The “Data as of” date in the status bar reflects the most recent refresh. Filings can post or be amended at any time, so very recent activity may not yet appear.

How the headline numbers are calculated

Total Raised
Sum of contributions reported for the period, net of committee-to-committee transfers so the same dollar isn't counted at two committees. The excluded transfer amount is disclosed on hover.
Total Spent / Borrowed
Sum of reported expenditures / loans for the period.
Raised vs Spent chart
Cumulative contributions and expenditures over time — these are flows, not a bank balance. The green dots are certified Cash on Hand and are the only true balance points.
Cash on Hand
The certified end-of-year balance from a filer's annual report (F102/F202). A balance is meaningless without its as-of date, so it is always shown “as of 12/31/<year>.”
Est. Current Cash
A hybrid estimate: the certified balance plus contributions and minus expenditures reported since that filing. Excludes loans, refunds, transfers, and in-kind. An estimate, not a certified figure.
Party label
Marked ✓ when verified against Secretary of State records and ~ when matched heuristically by name (may be imperfect).

Limitations & disclaimer

These figures are self-reported by candidates and committees to the Louisiana Board of Ethics. They reflect what was reported — amendments, late filings, and any records not filed electronically may not be included.

  • The bulk data publishes the same contribution once per report filing; figures here are de-duplicated and are approximations, not audited totals.
  • About 0.3% of names map to more than one filer (two people sharing a name, or one person with several committees). We use exact filer identity where it's known; bare-name links and a few in-cycle lists may still merge them.
  • The “Raised vs Spent” line is a cumulative flow, not cash on hand. Only the certified green dots are true balances.
  • This is not an official record. Always verify against ethics.la.gov before relying on any figure. Each profile's “Verify” button links to the official source.

Glossary

Cash on Hand (COH)
Money a committee has available at the end of a reporting period, per its certified filing.
War chest
A large balance of unspent campaign cash a filer can deploy.
In-kind
A non-cash contribution of goods or services, valued at fair-market price.
Transfer
Money moved between committees. Excluded from headline “Raised” to avoid double-counting.
PAC / Committee
A political action committee or campaign committee that files campaign-finance reports.
F102 / F202
The Board of Ethics annual report forms (F102 for candidates, F202 for PACs) that certify Cash on Hand.
Filer number
The Board of Ethics' unique ID for a committee/filer — the most reliable identity, since names collide.
Cycle
A four-year election period; figures and filters are grouped by cycle.
